GL-iNet Setup
=======================

Refrences: 
    
    Step 1-3: https://docs.gl-inet.com/router/en/4/faq/first_time_setup/

    Step 4-7: https://forum.gl-inet.com/t/allow-remote-access-lan-how-does-this-work/25231
              https://www.reddit.com/r/GlInet/comments/1fpm9xz/how_do_i_change_the_ip/




Step 1 - Power On

    Plug in power cable.

    When powered on, your router will broudcast both 2.4G and 5G Wi-Fi signal with the SSID: GL-SFT1200-xxx and GL-SFT1200-xxx-5G.

Step 2 - Connect via Wi-Fi OR Connect via LAN

    Connect to the Wi-Fi called GL-SFT1200-xxx, and then input the default Wi-Fi password goodlife, which is also printed at the bottom of the router.

    OR

    Plug the cable connecting to your computer into LAN port.

Step 3 - Set Up Router

    Visit http://192.168.8.1 in your broswer to set up your router start by choosing your prefered language.

    NOTE: If you cannot access the web Admin Panel, please check: https://docs.gl-inet.com/router/en/4/faq/cannot_access_web_admin_panel/

    Set up your admin password to nepi#12345

    Note: Wi-Fi may turn off during the initialization, please make sure to reconnect to the router.

Step 4 - Configure your GL-iNet

    Go to Admin Panel > Wireless

    Modify 5GHz WiFi

        Wi-Fi Name (SSID): NEPI_DEVICE1_999999
        
        Wi-Fi Password: nepi#12345

    Go to Admin Panel > Network > LAN

    Set Router IP Address to 192.168.179.1

Step 6 - Connect your computer

    In step 2 if you connected via Wi-Fi disconnect from the Wi-Fi called GL-SFT1200-xxx.

    If you connected via LAN unplug the cable connecting to your computer into LAN port.

    Then connect to the Wi-Fi called NEPI_DEVICE1_999999, and then input the Wi-Fi password nepi#12345

Step 7 - Connect Raspberry Pi 5

    Plug the cable connecting to your Raspberry Pi 5 into your LAN port
